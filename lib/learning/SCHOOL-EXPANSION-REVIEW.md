## Publication blockers and honest limits

The collection is deliberately absent from `ACTIVITY_CATALOG` and no CMS seed/import or enabled grade was changed. A reviewed metadata field records the editorial pass only.

The twelve canvas tasks are blocked for publication until cells announce their actual colour and row/column, keyboard/screen-reader journeys are accepted and suitable non-colour alternatives are demonstrated. The current player's cell label only says a cell number and whether it is painted; colour-labelled palette controls alone do not make an existing pattern inspectable. Coordinate, reflection, colour-pattern and fraction tasks especially depend on this. These gaps are recorded in every draft's review limitations. This review did not modify that shared player.

Grades 1–2 use short first prompts, but reading burden, narration/phonics quality and independent choice remain untested with children. Many investigation choices still use generic numbered markers rather than original activity artwork. The common second step records a chosen optional next action, not a performed explanation. Saved canvas artwork records participation, not objective correctness. There are no new rhythm-performance or scientific-simulation engines here.

No independent Hindi educator, developmental specialist, accessibility user study, board reviewer or child usability study took place. Hindi was inspected editorially for meaning, arithmetic agreement, instruction clarity and wording; that is not a certified language review. School grade does not establish age, language skill or reading ability. No retention or learning improvement was measured.

Anekāntavāda perspectives remain in tension: optional creation supports autonomy but provides weaker evidence; narration helps some readers but device voice failure remains possible; low-cost shared canvas offers keyboard buttons but presently fails colour inspection; parent guidance adds safety without requiring private disclosure or blocking learning. Those trade-offs are recorded rather than presented as universally correct.

Validation: nine focused tests pass, including 36 independent numeric calculations checked in English/Hindi, roots, uncertainty bounds, beat positions, selected-language sound prompts, explicit experimental premises, disjoint fractions and publication limitations. Scoped lint passes. These checks do not constitute browser acceptance or publication approval.
