# Tara completion celebration

Requested app asset: one original 3-second 480x360 silent Tara celebration. Keep the existing companion silhouette and colors. A single gentle hop and a restrained deterministic confetti burst acknowledge participation, without mastery claims or reward pressure. Static opening and ending stay legible. The app must offer reduced-motion and quiet presentation, must not loop the clip, and must use the poster when video cannot load.

Rendering is covered by the user's explicit instruction to implement and deploy app animations. This brief is an app integration asset rather than a separately gated promotional video.

Registry search: `hyperframes catalog --query 'small celebration confetti burst' --json`, words tier. Selected `confetti`. Its CSS-variable particle approach is reused with 16 particles, a smaller spread and finite fall/fade. Original Tara SVG source: `components/ui/learning-companion.tsx`.
