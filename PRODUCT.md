# Vidya

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Children from Nursery, LKG and UKG through school, and their parents. India families first. English default with Hindi available. Learners revisit on phones, tablets and laptops.

## Product Purpose
A place to explore, practise and create with age-appropriate choices, saved progress and family support. Child autonomy and parent connection coexist.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Next.js, Neon and Clerk remain. Parent authentication is separate from child device codes. Exact placement determines content eligibility, not inferred age. Preserve legacy Grade 13 compatibility. No paid services or invented curriculum coverage. Both parents need separate logins to one shared child profile through explicit access approval.

## Brand Commitments
User chose a creative learning studio: warm white, ink-blue, colourful artwork, playful children and calm parents. Optional original 2D bird companion. No em dashes in new copy. Light default with dark/system preferences.

## Product Principles
- One clear next action, with room for independent choice.
- Creation and practice both matter.
- Parent reports describe observations, not ability scores.
- No punishment for absence and no blocking children for missed parent reviews.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard alternatives, labelled controls, visible focus, readable contrast, reduced-motion support, large touch targets and browser zoom. Sound starts from an intentional gesture and has visible mute and separate volume controls.

## Evidence on Hand
Existing learning activities, music instruments, visual playground, published library and family dashboard. Availability varies by exact placement. No verified retention or child usability claims.
