# Worldwide curriculum vision

User direction recorded on 2026-10-01.

Vidya's long-term ambition is free access to learning across every school curriculum and university curriculum worldwide. Initial India-focused work is a starting point. CBSE, ICSE and international Cambridge pathways, including IGCSE, are examples of the initial focus, not the boundary of the product.

This is a product direction, not a claim that Vidya already covers every curriculum, has university affiliations, or awards recognized qualifications. The public commitment remains: "One world. One app. Free learning for every child."

## Catalog and learning experience

- Let learners choose their curriculum explicitly. Do not derive it from nationality, language, location or age.
- Keep curriculum authority, qualification or pathway, syllabus version, level, subject and content provenance distinct. Do not assume grades, stages, courses or credits are interchangeable.
- For university coverage, retain institution, program, course, academic year and source references where applicable. An institution's inclusion must not imply endorsement or partnership.
- Show actual coverage and review status. Distinguish planned coverage, framework material and source-reviewed learning content. Never present an unsupported curriculum as ready.
- Provide independent adult learning alongside the parent and child experiences. Existing child accounts must not gain adult privileges or transfer monitoring permissions automatically.
- Keep source provenance, stable content identity and retained progress when curricula change. Map equivalent topics only when the mapping has been reviewed.

## Delivery and sponsorship

Continue fixing enrollment, family isolation, reliability and existing content quality through small verified releases. Broader catalog design must support global expansion without promising unbuilt content.

Sponsorship requests should explain the worldwide school-through-university ambition, then ask for a bounded first pilot with measurable learning-quality, accessibility and reliability outcomes. Proposed pilot scope is not evidence of current adoption. Do not imply that sponsorship has been awarded or that a provider or institution endorses Vidya.

The next universities, jurisdictions and qualifications to implement have not been selected. Current school scheme verification, content permissions, translations and curriculum review remain evidence requirements for each addition.
